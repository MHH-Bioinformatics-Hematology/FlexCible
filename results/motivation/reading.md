## Reading

Question. A published Hi-C predictor is only useful when it scores above what needs no model. The measured map of any other cell type, used as if it were the prediction for the target cell, and the mean of the other cells' maps (the consensus) are such trivial predictors. We scored both on exactly the pixels on which the published predictors were scored: every cell type that has a measured O/E map at the bin size (14 other cells at 25, 10 and 5 kb on the held-out chromosomes; the 27 other AML patients; the 4 other cells of Akita's data set), with the measures of the benchmark tables (Pearson, SCC, insulation r, boundary recall, rank-matched loop recall; the compartment measure is in the per-measure tables and in `summary.csv`). The maps are the O/E maps the models are trained on (`crosscell_oe`, `scripts/oe_proper.py`), so predictor and trivial predictor live in the same space. Predictions of the published methods are the stored ones; nothing was rerun.

Result 1, other cells. Over the 355 (predictor, target, bin size, measure) cases of the five main measures, 82 percent of the (target, other cell) pairs score above the published predictor and in 82 percent of the cases more than half of the other cells do. The share is 75 percent on the held-out chromosomes of the released predictors (165 cases), 76 percent for C.Origami on GM12878, 87 percent for the published predictors on AML patients (160 cases) and 60 percent against Akita on its own cell types (15 cases; four other cells only). By measure the share is 92 percent for loop recall, 89 percent for insulation r, 86 percent for boundary recall, 79 percent for Pearson and 78 percent for SCC (loop enrichment r in the AML windows 74 percent). By bin size (held-out chromosomes and C.Origami de novo): 66 percent at 5 kb, 80 percent at 10 kb and 79 percent at 25 kb.

Result 2, consensus. The consensus scores above the published predictor in 326 of the 355 cases (92 percent), and in 302 of the 310 cases that remain after the two families listed under the exceptions. It is above Epiphany, both Hi-cGAN GM12878 models, Hi-cGAN on K562 and IMR-90 with the GM12878 model, the released HiC-Reg cross-chromosome variants (except K562 at 5 kb), C.Origami on GM12878 (all measures, all bin sizes), Akita on both of its cell types (all five measures) and every published predictor on every AML patient (Pearson, SCC, insulation r, loop enrichment r; boundary recall on 4 of 8 patients against Akita, a tie in the other four).

Per method (share of other cells above the predictor, pooled over targets; consensus above in k of n target blocks), 25 / 10 / 5 kb:
- Epiphany (GM12878): 1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00 in Pearson and in SCC, consensus above in all blocks and measures.
- Hi-cGAN GM12878 model on GM12878: Pearson 1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00, SCC the same; on K562 1.00 / 1.00; on IMR-90 1.00 / 1.00. Loop recall at 5 kb 0.79. The Hi-cGAN IMR-90 model on IMR-90 is the exception: 0.14 and 0.29 (Pearson, SCC) at 25 kb, 0.93 at 10 kb; the consensus is above in both.
- HiC-Reg, cross-chromosome variants (two released variants pooled): Pearson 0.75 / 0.50 / 0.41, SCC 0.86 / 0.66 / 0.43, insulation r 1.00 / 0.95 / 0.79, boundary recall 0.98 at every bin size, loop recall 1.00.
- C.Origami on GM12878 with the authors' tracks (section 81): Pearson 0.71 / 0.50 / 0.00 (at 5 kb the best other cell, HMEC, ties with it at 0.649), SCC 0.86 / 0.79 / 0.29, insulation r 1.00 / 0.93 / 0.93, boundary recall 0.93 at all three, loop recall 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.64; the consensus is above in every measure (Pearson 0.833 / 0.809 / 0.746 against 0.721 / 0.711 / 0.649).
- Akita on GM12878 and HFF (413 regions, 2 kb): no other cell is above Akita in Pearson (0 of 8 pairs), 1 of 8 in SCC, 6 of 8 in insulation r (4 other cells per target), 5 of 8 in boundary recall and 4 of 8 in loop recall; the four-cell consensus is above Akita in all five measures for both targets (Pearson 0.612 against 0.551 for GM12878 and 0.715 against 0.626 for HFF) and above it in 64 percent (GM12878) and 80 percent (HFF) of the regions in Pearson (`akita413_region_wins.csv`). The other cells here are the four neighbours in a 5-cell set, and the score at 2 kb is dominated by the diagonal decay that a sequence model learns.
- AML patients (10 kb windows centred on AML-specific loops and stripes, 8 patients, 27 other patients): against C.Origami and Epiphany 97 to 100 percent of the other patients score above them in every measure; against Akita 83 percent in Pearson, 60 percent in SCC, 78 percent in insulation r, 22 percent in boundary recall and 4 percent in loop enrichment r (Akita's loop enrichment r median 0.34 against 0.24 for the other patients and 0.49 for their mean). The mean of the other patients is above all four predictors in 8 of 8 patients in Pearson, SCC, insulation r and loop enrichment r.

Exceptions. (1) C.Origami in its own cell type, IMR-90: no other cell and not the consensus scores above it in Pearson (0.942 / 0.916 / 0.825 against the consensus 0.876 / 0.860 / 0.806 at 25 / 10 / 5 kb; the best other cell is GM23248 in all three), SCC and insulation r at any bin size; the consensus is above it in boundary recall and loop recall at all three bin sizes and 93 percent (10 and 5 kb) and 14 percent (25 kb) of the other cells are above it in boundary recall. On GM12878, a cell it has not been trained on, C.Origami loses to the consensus everywhere, which places the advantage in the training cell type (section 81). (2) HiC-Reg cross-validated variants (the maps come from the same chromosomes as the test pixels): above every other cell and the consensus in Pearson and SCC for GM12878 and K562 at every bin size (and below them in boundary recall and loop recall). (3) HiC-Reg cross-chromosome and multi-cell variants for K562 at 5 kb (Pearson 0.781 and 0.791 against the consensus 0.674); at 25 kb the same variants are below the consensus. (4) In the AML windows Akita ties with the consensus in boundary recall in four patients (0.500 against 0.500 in 018 and 270, 0.400 against 0.400 in 773) and is above it in one (546: 0.400 against 0.333).

Caveats. The pixel set of each block is the set of pixels finite in the target and in every source (competitor, consensus and all other cells), so that the competitor is rescored on pixels that can differ from the older tables; the competitor values of the new blocks differ from the stored values by at most 0.057 in Pearson (mean 0.015), 0.060 in SCC, 0.113 in insulation r and 0.034 in boundary recall (`check_vs_stored.csv`; the pixel counts differ by -10 to +37 percent, because our models are not sources of these blocks and the other cells' unmappable bins are). Consensus of a training cell is the leave-one-out mean of the other 11 training cells, of K562, HMEC and HUVEC the mean of the 12 training cells, so that the cell itself is never part of its consensus; the other 14 maps of a target include the held-out cells K562, HMEC and HUVEC. Values are means over the held-out chromosomes of a block (2 to 3 chromosomes), as in the benchmark tables. The tables count the 14 other cells as independent although several are close (IMR-90 and GM23248, for example). The AML scores are medians over a fixed random sample of 400 windows per patient (seed 0, 398 to 400 scored). The loop measure of the AML windows is the correlation of loop enrichment (the AML table of section 73), not a recall; The HiC-Reg 10 kb blocks (chr14, chr17) use the released variants of the blocks of section 63 only. Not scored: C.Origami and Epiphany on cells other than their stated ones (retrained and pre-trained variants of sections 77 and 79 are outside this analysis by decision), Hi-cGAN on K562 at 5 kb (no prediction exists), Akita on cells without a head, and any predictor that was not part of the benchmark blocks.
