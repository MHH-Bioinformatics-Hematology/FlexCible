Question. The release checkpoint has to serve two uses: prediction with a consensus map from other cells as the reference input, and prediction from the ChIP tracks alone. We compare three one-model checkpoints on the chromosome-held-out test windows of the 12 training cells and on the 413 Akita test regions, where K562, HMEC and HUVEC are held-out cells: all12one (13 marks, reference input, track dropout; registry section 84), all12one_full (additionally DNA methylation as a track, the cohesin-fraction row and the depletion augmentation; section 86) and all12one_fullrd (all12one_full with the reference plane set to 0 in 30 per cent of the training windows; section 92). The two new checkpoints were also scored with the reference plane at 0, and the dedicated ChIP-only models (all12honest_drop, trained without any reference at one bin size each) serve as the comparison for that use.

What the methylation and cohesin additions cost. With the consensus, all12one_full is level with all12one on the test windows (mean over the 12 cells +0.001, 0.000, +0.001 and 0.000 at 2, 5, 10 and 25 kb, higher in 7 of 12 cells at every size). On the 413 Akita regions the training cells are 0.002 to 0.007 lower (2 kb 0.742 against 0.749, 25 kb 0.936 against 0.939) and the held-out cells 0.000 to 0.009 lower. The additions, which allow the model to represent cohesin loss, therefore cost at most a few thousandths of Pearson r.

What the reference dropout costs with the consensus. all12one_fullrd is 0.003 to 0.005 below all12one_full on the test windows (2 kb 0.761 against 0.766, 10 kb 0.915 against 0.919, 25 kb 0.941 against 0.944) and lower in 11 or 12 of the 12 cells at every size. On the 413 regions of the training cells the difference is 0.003 to 0.006. On the three held-out cells it is between -0.003 and +0.013 (higher at 2 kb, 10 kb and 25 kb, lower at 5 kb). All three checkpoints are above the consensus alone on the training cells at every bin size. On the held-out cells the means of the three checkpoints are within 0.015 of the consensus alone at every bin size (all12one_fullrd 0.422 against 0.418 at 2 kb, 0.643 against 0.632 at 5 kb, 0.899 against 0.894 at 25 kb); K562 is 0.03 to 0.05 above the consensus for all12one_fullrd at every size (0.669 against 0.629 at 2 kb, 0.925 against 0.897 at 25 kb), whereas HMEC and HUVEC lie at or below it.

What the reference dropout gives without the consensus. With the reference plane at 0, all12one_full falls to a mean r of 0.42 (2 kb) to 0.60 (25 kb) on the test windows, in agreement with the failure seen on the held-out-chromosome benchmark (section 91). all12one_fullrd keeps 0.63 (2 kb), 0.74 (5 kb), 0.80 (10 kb) and 0.81 (25 kb), which is 0.20 to 0.22 higher in every one of the 12 cells at every size. It is above the dedicated ChIP-only model in 12 of 12 cells at 5 and 10 kb (+0.033 and +0.028) and in 11 of 12 at 25 kb (+0.016); at 2 kb it is 0.009 below the dedicated model (0 of 12 cells higher). On the held-out cells the plane-at-0 result of all12one_fullrd is within 0.001 of the dedicated model at 2 kb and 0.008 to 0.017 above it at 5, 10 and 25 kb (3 of 3 cells). The single release checkpoint therefore replaces the dedicated ChIP-only models from 5 kb up. The ChIP-only use remains clearly below the use with the consensus (0.81 against 0.94 at 25 kb on the test windows).

Limits. One run of each checkpoint, so differences of a few thousandths are within what a second seed might change (not measured). The held-out group has three cells, of which HMEC and HUVEC come from single-lane maps and lie at or below the consensus for every model. The dedicated ChIP-only models were trained on the same 12 cells and scored on the same windows and regions; the plane at 0 in the one model equals the zero reference plane that fullrd saw in training.
