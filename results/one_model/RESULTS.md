## Reading

The one model matches the dedicated models. On the test windows of the 12 training cells the median over cells is +0.004 (5 kb), +0.004 (10 kb) and +0.004 (25 kb) above the dedicated model, with the one model higher in 12 of 12, 12 of 12 and 11 of 12 cells; at 2 kb it is 0.004 lower (higher in 1 of 12 cells). Both beat the leave-one-out consensus alone in 12 of 12 cells at every bin size. On the 413 Akita regions the training cells give the same picture (+0.001 at 5 kb, +0.007 at 10 kb, +0.005 at 25 kb, -0.004 at 2 kb). For the held-out cells K562, HMEC and HUVEC the paired median differences over the regions lie between -0.012 (HUVEC, 2 kb) and +0.009 (HUVEC, 5 kb); the bootstrap interval excludes zero in favour of the dedicated model for all three cells at 2 kb (-0.003 to -0.012) and in favour of the one model for K562 at 25 kb (+0.005), HMEC at 10 kb (+0.004) and HUVEC at 5 kb (+0.009), and for HUVEC at 25 kb against it (-0.003). The single-lane HMEC and HUVEC maps are noisy and score at or below the 12-cell consensus at 2 to 10 kb for both model kinds. One network for four bin sizes therefore costs at most 0.01 at 2 kb and gains up to 0.007 at 10 and 25 kb.

Caveats. Same checkpoint selection for both (honest validation, patience 10); one training run each (no seed spread); the held-out median is over three cells, K562, HMEC and HUVEC (the manuscript's five-cell median also contains the deep HMEC and HUVEC maps). Registry section 88.
