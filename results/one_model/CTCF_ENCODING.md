# Encodings of CTCF removal on the release candidate all12one_fullrd

Registry section 100, 2026-11-01. Generated by `scripts/ctcf_encoding_report.py`; tables in `results/one_model/ctcf_encoding/ENCODINGS_degron.{csv,md}` and `ENCODINGS_hap1.{csv,md}`.

## Reading

Question. The release checkpoint answered the measured CTCF degron of HCT116 very differently under two encodings of "CTCF removed": raw CTCF signal set to zero (change-map Pearson 0.14, 57.5% of the loops weaker, no consensus, 10 kb) and the CTCF track declared absent (0.38 / 0.47 at 10 / 25 kb, 94% / 90% weaker; 0.44 / 0.52 and 99% / 96% with the consensus). We tested whether the motif rows MA0139.2 (forward and reverse hit rows, from which the two directed planes are built) keep the anchors in place when only the ChIP is removed, and whether removing them as well makes the model reproduce the degron.

1. Zero signal and absent track are the same input. `TrackNormalizer.absent_values` is the normalised value of a raw-zero track, so a declared-absent CTCF track and a zero CTCF track enter the network with identical values. The predictions confirm it: with `flexcible predict` the cool files of zero CTCF and absent CTCF (and of the other two pairs) agree in every one of 40.6 million pixels (maximum difference 0.0), and both give Pearson 0.141. The encodings b and c, and a and d, are therefore one row each in the table. The difference of 0.14 against 0.38 does not come from the encoding.

2. The difference comes from the CTCF ChIP that was given to the model. Sections 21, 95 and 99 predict HCT116 from the ENCODE set `hg38_hct116` (`crosscell_predict`); `scripts/perturb_degron_zero.py` uses the config sample hct116 of the release checkpoint, whose ChIP is the set of the untreated degron cells (`perturb_chip/untreated`, 4DN fold change). The two CTCF tracks correlate at 0.89 (log1p, 10 kb bins, chr1 20 to 120 Mb) but differ in range: the 99th percentile is 6.2 for the degron-cell track and 2.6 for the ENCODE track, the maximum 15.6 and 6.3. The per-region pipeline of section 99 run with the degron-cell ChIP (P2) gives 0.149 / 0.161 at 10 / 25 kb without the consensus and 58% / 62% of the loops weaker, which is the zero-signal result of the tiled pipeline (P3: 0.141 / 0.179, 57.5% / 60.5%). The same pipeline with the ENCODE ChIP (P1) reproduces section 99 exactly (0.380 / 0.471, 94.2% / 89.6%; consensus 0.444 / 0.520). The position of the window does not matter: shifting the 1.92 Mb window by 40 bins either way against the region centre leaves the no-consensus 10 kb result at 0.375 and 0.382 (0.380 centred). With the consensus and the degron-cell ChIP the CTCF removal is read in the wrong direction (Pearson -0.09 / -0.14, 23% / 29% of the loops weaker): the consensus holds the loops in place. Why the degron-cell track behaves so differently was not tested; its sharper and higher peaks are the candidate (a clipped or rescaled track would test it).

3. Removing the motif rows does not reproduce the degron. With the ENCODE ChIP, CTCF removed plus motif rows zero (a) is as good as CTCF removed alone (b) or slightly worse: 0.377 against 0.380 at 10 kb and 0.448 against 0.471 at 25 kb without the consensus, 0.419 against 0.444 and 0.490 against 0.520 with it; the fraction of weaker loops is unchanged or lower (93.3% against 94.2%, 95.7% against 98.7%), while the median loop change grows at 10 kb without consensus (-0.286 against -0.221, measured -0.489). With the degron-cell ChIP, which has the poor baseline, the motif rows account for a small part: 0.141 to 0.161 and 0.179 to 0.235 without the consensus, loops weaker 57.5% to 67.8% (10 kb) and 60.5% to 67.8% (25 kb), median loop change -0.020 to -0.046; with the consensus the change is -0.080 to -0.052 and 24% to 23%. The motif rows alone with the CTCF ChIP intact (e1) give a small response (Pearson 0.01 to 0.15 in P1 and P2, loops weaker 42% to 65%) that equals the H3K36me3 control with the ENCODE ChIP without the consensus and exceeds it by 0.04 to 0.21 in the other settings, so the model reads the motif rows weakly, as sites that carry some of the loop signal on their own, and the motif evidence is not what keeps the anchors in place after CTCF removal. The hypothesis is supported at most in a minor part for the degron-cell ChIP and not for the ENCODE ChIP.

4. Controls. Removing H3K36me3 gives 0.02 to 0.09 without the consensus in every pipeline (identical for zero and absent), loops weaker 50% to 60% and a median loop change of at most 0.009 in absolute value; with the consensus it is -0.05 to -0.19. CTCF removal exceeds this control by 0.31 to 0.38 (P1, no consensus) and by 0.11 (P3, no consensus, the figure input) at 10 and 25 kb. APA of the unperturbed prediction is 1.12 to 1.42, of the perturbed prediction 1.03 to 1.42, against 1.70 and 1.35 (10 kb) and 1.43 and 1.26 (25 kb) measured.

5. HAP1 single-site deletions. The protocol of section 99 already set the CTCF signal and the motif rows MA0139.2 to zero in the deleted bin (site +-1 kb, `scripts/perturb_hap1/run_predictions.py`), so the requested variant "signal zero and motif rows zero" is that protocol and its numbers are unchanged (section 99). The contrast variant, signal zero with the motif rows left in place, gives the same result: all 12 loops decreased in both, insulation direction 16/16 and 15/16 at 10 and 5 kb in both, control site at most 0.013, mean predicted change at the 10 convergent loops -0.300 against -0.289 (10 kb) and -0.475 against -0.466 (5 kb) without the consensus, -0.095 against -0.097 and -0.109 against -0.110 with it (measured -1.10 and -1.25). A single deleted motif is therefore represented by the CTCF signal alone; the motif rows add at most 0.01.

Consequence for the manuscript. The degron analysis has to name its input: with the ENCODE HCT116 ChIP the release checkpoint reproduces the degron (Pearson 0.38 / 0.47 without and 0.44 / 0.52 with the consensus; 94% / 90% and 99% / 96% of the loops weaker), with the ChIP of the untreated degron cells it does not (0.14 / 0.18, 58% / 61%). The existing figure script reads the zero-signal cools (degron-cell ChIP) in its default and the section 99 files (ENCODE ChIP) with `absent`, so its two variants differ by the input track, not by the encoding. The motif-zero files `results/perturb_degron_zero/all12one_fullrd_{noref,cons}_motifzero` follow the layout of `all12one_fullrd_noref` (degron-cell ChIP, P3, encoding a) and change the figure by one path; they raise the zero-signal result only to 0.16 / 0.24. Limits: one checkpoint; the 25 kb absent runs were not repeated in the tiled pipeline because absent and zero are identical there (checked at 10 kb); the consensus is the leave-one-out map of HCT116; single run per setting, no seeds.
