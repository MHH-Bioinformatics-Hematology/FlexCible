Question. Registry 96 found that without the consensus the release model reads loops from CTCF and, where CTCF is missing, from cohesin. The coordinator's hypothesis for the gap between the two HCT116 ChIP sets was that the ENCODE set `hg38_hct116` lacks SMC3 and has only a weak RAD21 track, so nothing marks the anchors once CTCF is removed, while the degron-batch set has RAD21 and SMC3 that keep the anchors.

Inventory. Both sets hold the same 12 of the 13 marks: CTCF, RAD21, H3K27ac, H3K27me3, H3K36me3, H3K4me1, H3K4me2, H3K4me3, H3K79me2, H3K9ac, H3K9me3 and H4K20me1. SMC3 is missing from both (`missing.json` of `hg38_hct116` and `missing_tracks: SMC3` of the config sample); Meth comes from a separate folder in both uses. The mark subset therefore cannot explain the gap, and experiment (h) is the degron-batch row of section A (P2: 0.150 / 0.161 without the consensus). The tracks differ in amplitude. RAD21 (chr1 20 to 120 Mb, 10 kb bins): mean 0.85 against 0.55, 99th percentile 6.06 against 1.14, maximum 17.5 against 3.0 (degron batch against ENCODE); CTCF: 99th percentile 6.19 against 2.63, maximum 15.6 against 6.3.

Experiments (per-region pipeline, rows of the table below). f: degron-batch set, CTCF, RAD21 (and SMC3) removed; g: RAD21 (and SMC3) removed with CTCF intact; i: ENCODE set with the RAD21 track of the degron batch; j: ENCODE set with the CTCF track of the degron batch; k: degron-batch set with the ENCODE CTCF track (i to k are additions to the requested list that separate the two tracks). The tiled pipeline repeats f and g.

Results.
1. Without the consensus the cohesin track is what keeps the anchors in the degron-batch set. Removing RAD21 together with CTCF (f) moves the response to the ENCODE level: Pearson 0.352 / 0.352 at 10 / 25 kb (0.357 / 0.371 tiled) against 0.150 / 0.161 with CTCF removed alone, loops weaker 92.1% / 74.1% (tiled 93.6% / 75.0%) against 58.4% / 62.1%, median loop change -0.225 / -0.107 against -0.020 / -0.019; the ENCODE set gives 0.380 / 0.471, 94.2% / 89.6%, -0.221 / -0.137. At 10 kb f reaches the ENCODE result, at 25 kb about three quarters of its Pearson.
2. RAD21 removal alone (g, CTCF intact) makes 70.4% of the loops weaker at 10 kb (46.5% at 25 kb) with a median change of -0.043 (0.004), while the Pearson with the measured change stays low (0.076 / -0.071); the model does read loops from cohesin, but removing it does not look like the CTCF degron. With the consensus g is negative (-0.151 / -0.304).
3. Swapping single tracks into the ENCODE set shows two contributions, both without the consensus: the degron-batch RAD21 (i) lowers the response from 0.380 / 0.471 to 0.286 / 0.408 (loops weaker 84.6% / 86.9%), the degron-batch CTCF (j) to 0.233 / 0.288 (78.1% / 74.0%); the reverse swap (k, ENCODE CTCF in the degron set) gives 0.262 / 0.426 (81.5% / 87.6%). With the degron-batch CTCF the response at 10 kb falls from 0.380 to 0.233 and, with both tracks of the degron batch, to 0.150, so the effects are about additive (-0.09 for RAD21, -0.15 for CTCF at 10 kb).
4. With the consensus the cohesin track is not the cause. The RAD21 swap leaves the result unchanged (i: 0.438 / 0.577 against 0.444 / 0.520), removing RAD21 as well repairs little (f: 0.063 / 0.042 against -0.089 / -0.137), while the degron-batch CTCF alone turns the response around (j: -0.275 / -0.211, 27.7% / 38.8% of the loops weaker); k gives 0.282 / 0.519. The consensus reproduces the wild-type loops unless the CTCF track says that CTCF is gone, and the degron-batch CTCF track does not carry that information well. Why was not tested.
5. The H3K36me3 control stays near zero or positive in these sets without the consensus (0.02 to 0.20) and negative with it in the sets with the degron-batch marks (-0.15 to -0.20), so the swaps do not change the control reading of section A.

Reading. The hypothesis holds in part and with a different premise. The ChIP sets contain the same marks; the gap without the consensus is the amplitude of the cohesin and CTCF tracks. A strong RAD21 track of the untreated cell keeps the loops after CTCF removal, because in the real degron cohesin is still on the chromatin but the model, which reads loops from cohesin where CTCF is missing, then keeps them; in the experiment the loops weaken. The agreement of the ENCODE set with the degron therefore depends partly on its weak RAD21 track, and f (RAD21 removed as well) is an in-silico test and not a faithful degron encoding. With the consensus the CTCF track of the degron batch is the cause. Limits: SMC3 absent in all sets, one checkpoint, one run per setting, the tiled pipeline has f and g only.
