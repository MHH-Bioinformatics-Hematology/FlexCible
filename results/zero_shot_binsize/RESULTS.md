# Untrained bin sizes: the one model at 1, 4, 20 and 50 kb

Generated by `scripts/zero_shot_binsize.py` (registry section 89); table in `summary.csv`.

Setup. The shared checkpoint `all12one` (trained at 2, 5, 10 and 25 kb, continuous log2(bin size) embedding, reference input, track dropout) predicts GM12878 (a training cell; reference = leave-one-out consensus of the other training cells) and K562 (held out; reference = consensus of the training cells) at bin sizes it never saw: 4 and 20 kb lie inside the trained range, 1 and 50 kb outside. Maps: 4DN mcools (GM12878, IMR-90, HCT116, HFF, H1, K562) and the ENCODE cools of the seven other training cells, coarsened from the finest zoom that divides the bin size, restricted to chr2, chr3 and chr10 (chr2 and chr10 at 1 kb) and a distance band, ICE balanced (cooler, ignore_diags 2, 500 iterations), then `scripts/oe_proper.py` (validated: 10 kb maps rebuilt this way correlate with the training maps at median r 0.985 per region on chr10) and `scripts/consensus_loo.py`. Scored on the Akita test regions on those chromosomes (80 regions; 62 at 1 kb) by `scripts/crosscell_predict.py` (single window per region, reverse-complement averaging), against the measured map of the same cell. `nearest_trained_median_r` is the same model at the nearest trained bin size (log scale: 2 kb for 1 kb, 5 kb for 4 kb, 25 kb for 20 and 50 kb) on the same regions, scored on that bin size's grid; it is not the same task, because coarser bins are easier, so it marks the level reached where the model was trained.

Normalisers. `nearest`: track and contact normalisers of the nearest trained bin size (the default of `flexcible predict --bin-size`). `refit_tracks`: track statistics refitted on the cell's own signal at the new bin size (what `--renormalize-tracks` does). `refit_all`: track and contact normalisers from `flexcible precompute` at the new bin size (pooled over both cells). Window sizes 1024, 512, 96 and 48 bins at 1, 4, 20 and 50 kb (1.02 to 2.4 Mb).

Reduced consensus at 1 kb. The ENCODE .hic files of the seven non-4DN training cells were deleted after conversion at 2 kb and coarser, so the 1 kb references are means of the 4DN training cells only: 4 other cells for GM12878, 5 for K562 (`n_consensus_cells`). The 1 kb consensus is therefore weaker than at the other bin sizes (11 and 12 cells), which lowers the baseline and the model alike.

Reading. At every untrained bin size and for both cells the model with the nearest-size normalisers is above the consensus alone: GM12878 0.587 against 0.500 (1 kb), 0.889 against 0.774 (4 kb), 0.932 against 0.850 (20 kb), 0.896 against 0.881 (50 kb); K562 0.462 against 0.416, 0.798 against 0.740, 0.933 against 0.894, 0.923 against 0.910. The gain is largest inside the trained range (4 and 20 kb, +0.04 to +0.12) and shrinks at 50 kb (+0.013 to +0.015), where the consensus alone is already close to the measured map. At 20 kb the model reaches the level of the neighbouring trained size (0.932 and 0.933 against 0.935 and 0.940 at 25 kb); at 4 kb it is 0.026 (GM12878) and 0.028 (K562) below 5 kb. At 1 kb the absolute level is low (0.59, 0.46) against 0.77 and 0.62 at 2 kb, with the weaker consensus as one cause that is not separable here.

Refitted normalisers. Refitting the track statistics (`--renormalize-tracks`) does not help systematically: against the nearest-size normalisers the median r changes by -0.014 to +0.029 (+0.029 for GM12878 at 1 kb; -0.014 for GM12878 at 20 kb, -0.011 for K562 at 4 kb; the other five changes lie between -0.009 and 0.000). Refitting the contact normaliser as well (`refit_all`) changes it by -0.013 to +0.032 (positive only for GM12878 at 1 kb, +0.032, and 50 kb, +0.006). The z-scores of fold-change signal averaged per bin transfer across bin sizes, so `--renormalize-tracks` is needed for tracks from another pipeline, not for another bin size.

Caveats. Single window per region and 80 regions on three chromosomes, so the medians carry about +-0.01 sampling noise; GM12878 is a training cell (its Hi-C was seen at 2 to 25 kb, never at these bin sizes). Maps at the new bin sizes were balanced on the band-limited cis matrix, which differs slightly from the 4DN weights used for the training maps.
